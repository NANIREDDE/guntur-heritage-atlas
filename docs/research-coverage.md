## Current source policy

Government district and tourism pages are useful starting sources for identifying places and documenting what the district administration publishes. They are not automatically treated as conclusive evidence for every historical or traditional claim.

## Next evidence layers

1. Archaeological and protected-monument records.
2. Epigraphic and inscriptional evidence.
3. Census, gazetteer and archival material.
4. Academic publications and historical scholarship.
5. Temple or institutional records where available.
6. Carefully labeled oral/local traditions.

A record should remain `partial-verification` or `needs-review` when important claims cannot yet be independently supported.
