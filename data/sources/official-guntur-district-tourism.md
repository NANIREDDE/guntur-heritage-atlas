# Official Guntur District Tourism Source

- Publisher: District Administration, Guntur, Government of Andhra Pradesh
- Page: Tourism
- URL: https://guntur.ap.gov.in/tourism/
- Retrieved: 2026-09-16

## Relevant evidence

The district tourism page identifies temples in Mangalagiri, Ponnur and Pedakakani among prominent tourist places. It also describes the historical prominence of Undavalli caves and Kondaveedu fort.

## Research rule

This source is used as an authoritative government tourism source for the existence/description of listed attractions. It is not, by itself, sufficient evidence for every historical claim about a temple. More specific claims should receive their own source records.
